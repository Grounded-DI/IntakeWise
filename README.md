# IntakeWise DI² - Intake QC / Validation Demonstration

## Overview

IntakeWise DI² is a Grounded DI LLC intake-review workflow support module. This repository preserves one licensing and pilot-readiness demonstration for a commercial-property water-loss / mold-exposure intake. The public artifact shows how source-linked chronology, contradiction review, missing-information flags, escalation routing, confidence classification, structured export, and an audit capsule can be assembled before downstream claims, legal, coverage, compliance, or operations decisions.

This is a public demonstration of a sample intake packet. It is not an actual case, client matter, production deployment, legal opinion, coverage determination, or replacement for human review.

## What the artifact demonstrates

The PDF records a source-linked review of five intake sources:

- intake call summary;
- maintenance report;
- insurance intake form;
- vendor mitigation invoice; and
- text-message export.

The workflow produces a chronology, contradiction matrix, unsupported-statement review, missing-information register, escalation recommendation, confidence-routing summary, structured export packet, and audit capsule.

The strongest supported findings in the sample are:

- a water-loss event involving the kitchen-sink area occurred;
- the record contains a prior-leak signal before the reported March 2 event;
- a vendor arrived on March 2 at 8:05 AM and documented standing water; and
- mitigation services were documented.

The review keeps disputed or unsupported matters visible. It records conflicts about the sudden-burst narrative, shutoff time, damage-discovery date, occupancy, sink-use warning, mitigation timing, insurance-form accuracy, and ceiling-damage causation. Mold causation is marked **insufficient data**, and coverage posture is marked **needs review**.

## Technical significance

The useful control point is early risk visibility. IntakeWise preserves source conflicts instead of smoothing them into one narrative, flags unsupported statements rather than treating them as facts, and routes the packet for targeted document follow-up. That gives claims, legal, compliance, and operations reviewers a structured explanation of what is known, what is disputed, and what must be obtained before downstream work proceeds.
